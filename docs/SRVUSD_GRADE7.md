# SRVUSD Grade 7 Curriculum Profile

ShareCapsule Prepare has a local academic profile for San Ramon Valley Unified School District Grade 7 Math and Science.

## Activation

The specialized model is selected when the learner profile contains:

- Academic / Exam track
- Grade 7
- San Ramon Valley Unified School District (`SRVUSD`) or a recognized San Ramon middle-school context
- Mathematics or Science

The Academic intake includes presets for:

1. Course 2 Math
2. Course 3 Math
3. Grade 7 Integrated / Life Science

The school name is optional and is used only to refine public-source research and keep progress reports separated by local target.

## Mathematics

SRVUSD public curriculum material states that middle-school mathematics uses Courses 1, 2, and 3 from the California mathematics framework/Common Core pathway and that students in these courses access SpringBoard Mathematics. Course 2 is the typical Grade 7 placement; Course 3 is available through advancement.

### Course 2 Math competencies

- Ratios & proportional relationships
  - unit rates
  - proportional relationships
  - scale drawings
- Percent & rational numbers
  - percent equations
  - markup/discount
  - simple interest
  - integer/rational-number operations
- Expressions & equations
  - algebraic expressions
  - linear equations
  - inequalities and real-world modeling
- Geometry & measurement
  - geometric constructions and angle relationships
  - scale factors
  - area, surface area, volume
- Statistics & probability
  - population inference
  - comparing populations
  - probability models and chance processes

### Course 3 Math competencies

- Real numbers & exponents
  - integer exponents
  - scientific notation
  - irrational/real numbers
- Linear functions & systems
  - equations in one and two variables
  - graphing, slope
  - systems of linear equations
- Transformations & similarity
  - congruence
  - similarity
  - transformations/dilations
- Pythagorean theorem
- 3D geometry & volume
  - cylinders
  - cones
  - spheres

## Science

SRVUSD's district science page identifies Inspire Science as the core grades 6–8 material and links the NGSS/state science framework. School course descriptions can vary in how units are organized, so Prepare uses the configured Grade 7 unit map below as the shared preparation profile and lets live public research refine it when a school is named.

### Grade 7 Science competencies

- Cellular & body systems
  - cells as units of life
  - photosynthesis/cellular respiration
  - interactions among organism subsystems
- Weather, climate & Earth systems
  - interacting Earth systems
  - local weather
  - regional climate
- Genetics, adaptation & inheritance
  - trait transmission
  - genetic variation
  - natural selection
  - population adaptation
- Human impact & ecosystems
  - greenhouse gases
  - ecosystem changes
  - natural-resource conservation
- Scientific inquiry & evidence
  - questions and models
  - investigations
  - interpreting data
  - evidence-based explanations

## Diagnostic behavior

Each curriculum path currently has at least ten original diagnostic items. The engine seeds one question per competency, then can use a second question in the same competency as an adaptive follow-up or micro-retest.

The questions are original ShareCapsule Prepare items; the service does not copy district textbook questions.

## Privacy / progress reports

District, optional school, and curriculum placement are included in the signed report target identity. Raw diagnostic answers remain excluded from the progress report. This keeps Course 2, Course 3, and school-specific science progress histories from being mixed together.
